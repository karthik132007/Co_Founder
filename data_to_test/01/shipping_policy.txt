SHIPPING AND RETURNS POLICY
Document Version: 2.0

Policy Owner: Accounts and Compliance Department

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Shipping and Returns Policy governs the dispatch, delivery, cancellation, and return of all products manufactured and sold by Indian Herbs Pvt. Ltd., including but not limited to Kummudai Tailem, Ubtan Face Pack, Herbal Aloe Gel, Neem Face Wash, Sandalwood Cream, and Ayurvedic Body Oil. This Policy applies to all purchases made through our official website, e-commerce partners such as Amazon India and Flipkart, wholesale and retail distributors, and export partners. For any questions not addressed here, please refer to our Product Information File or contact the Accounts Department directly.

2. SHIPPING POLICY

2.1 Domestic Shipping within India

The shipping partners used are Delhivery, Ecom Express, and India Post for remote areas. Orders are processed within 24 to 48 business hours of order confirmation. Delivery lead times are as follows: retail website and e‑commerce orders take 2 to 5 business days, wholesale orders take 5 to 7 business days, and bulk institutional orders take 7 to 10 business days. Shipping charges are a flat rate of 49 rupees for all orders below 999 rupees. Shipping is free for all orders of 999 rupees and above, inclusive of taxes. For wholesale and export orders, shipping charges are calculated separately based on volume and destination. A tracking link will be shared via email and SMS within 24 hours of dispatch.

2.2 International Shipping for Export

We ship to select international markets through our authorised export partners, currently servicing the USA, UK, UAE, Singapore, and Australia. The lead time is 15 to 20 business days, subject to customs clearance. Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on weight, dimensions, and destination country. Important note: import duties, taxes, and customs clearance fees are not included in the product price or shipping fee. These charges, if applicable, are to be borne solely by the customer. Indian Herbs Pvt. Ltd. has no control over these charges and cannot predict their amount. We comply with all export regulations. Products containing certain herbal extracts may be restricted in some countries; it is the buyer’s responsibility to check local import regulations before placing an order.

3. ORDER CANCELLATION POLICY

For cancellations before dispatch, a full refund of 100 percent of the order value will be processed within 3 to 5 business days. After dispatch or while the order is in transit, cancellation is not accepted once the order has been handed over to the shipping partner. The customer may refuse delivery; however, return shipping charges will be deducted from the refund amount. For wholesale or bulk orders, cancellation must be requested in writing to the Accounts Department at least 48 hours before the scheduled dispatch date. A cancellation fee of 10 percent of the invoice value may apply.

4. RETURNS POLICY

We take great pride in the quality of our Ayurvedic formulations. However, if you are not entirely satisfied, we offer a straightforward return process under the following conditions.

4.1 Return Window
You may request a return within 7 calendar days from the date of delivery. The return request must be submitted via email to compliance at indianherbs dot example with your order ID and reason for return.

4.2 Eligibility Criteria for Return
A product that is unused, unopened, and in its original amber glass bottle with dropper and outer packaging is acceptable for a full refund. A product that is damaged during transit, such as broken bottle, leakage, or tampered seal, is acceptable for a full refund plus return shipping reimbursement, provided you give photo or video evidence within 48 hours of delivery. A product that is opened or used, even once, is not acceptable for return for hygiene reasons. A product that is past its expiry date or from an expired batch due to a manufacturing defect is acceptable for a full refund; our Quality Assurance team will verify batch records. A return requested after 7 days of delivery is not acceptable except in cases of proven manufacturing defects.

4.3 Return Process
To initiate a return, email compliance at indianherbs dot example with your Order ID, product name, and reason, attaching supporting images for damaged or defective items. Our team will review your request within 2 business days and share a Return Authorization Number and return address. Then pack the product securely in its original packaging. Return shipping costs are borne by the customer unless the product is defective or damaged during transit, in which case we will reimburse up to 100 rupees for domestic returns. Upon receipt, our quality assurance team will inspect the product within 3 to 5 business days.

4.4 Refund Timeline and Mode
Approved refunds will be processed within 7 to 10 business days from the date of inspection. Refunds for prepaid orders will be credited back to the original payment method, such as credit card, debit card, UPI, or net banking. For cash on delivery orders, refunds will be issued via bank transfer to the customer’s bank account, and bank details must be provided. Wholesale and bulk order refunds will be processed via the original banking channel, NEFT or RTGS, as per our vendor payment policy.

5. DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS – SPECIAL HANDLING

If you receive a product with a broken seal, leakage, or any visible defect, do not discard the original packaging or the product. Send clear photographs of the outer box, inner packaging, and the product defect to compliance at indianherbs dot example within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange for a replacement or full refund at no extra cost, including a reimbursement for the return shipping charges. For products like Kummudai Tailem, the amber glass bottle is designed to protect the herbal formulation from light. If the dropper is broken but the seal is intact, please contact us for a partial replacement of the dropper.

6. EXCHANGE POLICY

We do not offer direct exchanges. If you wish to exchange a product, please return the original item as per the Returns Policy and place a new order separately.
