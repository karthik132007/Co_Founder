PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

Herbal Hair Oil is a traditional Ayurvedic formulation designed to promote healthy hair growth, reduce hair fall, prevent premature greying, and nourish the scalp. The oil is prepared using the classical Ayurvedic method of blending potent herbs with natural carrier oils. This herbal preparation is formulated to strengthen hair roots, improve hair texture, add natural shine, and soothe dry and itchy scalps. It is suitable for all hair types including dry, oily, normal, and chemically treated hair.

KEY INGREDIENTS

The key ingredients include Bhringraj which is known for promoting hair growth and reducing hair fall. Amla is rich in vitamin C and natural antioxidants that prevent greying and strengthen follicles. Brahmi is used for calming the scalp and reducing stress‑induced hair loss. Hibiscus flowers and leaves provide natural conditioning and add shine. Neem has antibacterial and antifungal properties that help control dandruff and scalp infections. Coconut oil serves as a deep penetrating base oil that moisturises the hair shaft. Sesame oil is used as a secondary base oil to improve blood circulation to the scalp. Curry leaves are included to restore hair pigments and delay greying. Aloe vera soothes the scalp and reduces inflammation.

USAGE INSTRUCTIONS

For recommended usage, warm the oil slightly by placing the bottle in warm water for a few minutes. Apply a sufficient amount of oil to the scalp and gently massage with fingertips in circular motions for 5 to 10 minutes to stimulate blood circulation. Leave the oil on for at least 30 minutes. For best results, leave it on overnight. Wash your hair with a mild herbal shampoo and rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Use the oil at least twice a week for optimal results. For very dry or damaged hair, you may use it three times a week. Consistency is key and regular use for 4 to 6 weeks will show visible improvement in hair texture, shine, and reduction in hair fall.
